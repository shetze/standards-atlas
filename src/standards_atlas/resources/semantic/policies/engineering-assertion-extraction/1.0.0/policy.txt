R01 — Model the engineering-relevant meaning of the target clause, not every mentioned phrase. Treat the target as structurally embedded: its own source-backed heading is content, and justified hierarchical or sequential context may be necessary to understand it.
R02 — Keep explicit engineering work products and evidence objects as first-class entities when they belong to the meaning core; do not hide them only inside requirement labels or literals.
R03 — When the source actually requires an artefact, preserve the requirement-to-work-product relation when the closed ontology can express it. Mentioning or defining an artefact is not automatically a requirement to produce it.
R04 — A requirement to record information may be represented by an EngineeringRecord. Do not invent a separate plan, report, file or organisational document unless the supplied source supports that meaning.
R05 — Choose the most appropriate class from the supplied closed ontology based on meaning, not keywords or convenience. Keep requirements, activities, work products and concrete project artefacts conceptually distinct.
R06 — Emit only source-supported assertions with correct predicate, direction and arguments. Entities do not need artificial edges merely to make the graph dense; an entity-only result can be correct.
R07 — Handle lists by semantic independence. Split genuinely independent requirements or objects; otherwise preserve a coherent list as one meaning unit. Formatting alone does not decide granularity.
R08 — Do not mechanically atomize Notes, Examples or illustrative details. They may inform interpretation, but do not turn examples into general obligations without source support.
R09 — Definitions may be represented by typed entities without a defining assertion. Do not manufacture normative relations solely because a term is defined.
R10 — Preserve conditions, exceptions, exclusions, negation, scope restrictions and relevant temporal qualifications. If no suitable relation exists, preserve essential qualification in the normalized label instead of dropping it or inventing a predicate.
R11 — Interpret headings, ancestors, introductions, details and exceptions only where their content and structural role justify semantic reach. Context availability or adjacency alone never authorizes copying an independent claim into the target result.
R12 — Determine normative force from the actual statement and its justified structural frame. Predicate names, clause-type labels and confidence are not substitutes for force analysis; a relation such as requires may be informative.
R13 — Keep semantic normalization separate from evidence. Evidence must point to exact supplied source surfaces, keep each source owner and span distinct, and support the claimed meaning. An exact quote proves occurrence, not every interpretation attached to it.
R14 — Preserve local semantic identity without targeting Golden IDs. Generated candidates must be derived from the current target and supplied source package; do not introduce clause-ID-specific rules, memorized expected outputs or cross-clause identity merges.
