1. Definition versus production requirement: a source that defines “validation evidence” as objective information supports a WorkProduct-like entity, but no requires assertion unless the supplied source also says that such evidence shall be produced or provided.
2. Independent list versus descriptive list: separate “shall” bullets that impose different integrity/mode constraints may justify separate requirements; a list that merely enumerates strategies belonging to one requirement may remain one requirement with one summarized specifies assertion.
3. Informative relation versus normative force: descriptive guidance can state that an integration activity commonly requires an assumption check. If represented as requires, its normative_force remains informative rather than being upgraded from the predicate name.
4. Structural frame versus copied claim: an ancestor heading or introductory sibling may establish that following checks concern a named plan or review objective. Use that frame when the target check depends on it, but do not copy another sibling’s independent requirement merely because both surfaces were supplied.
5. Condition preservation: “the rationale shall be recorded only when the option is excluded” supports a conditional recording requirement and a rationale record. Preserve “only when the option is excluded”; do not generalize it into an unconditional recording obligation.
