Decision 1 — clause or requirement applicability
Set contains_clause_or_requirement_applicability to true only when at least one explicit statement governs whether this clause, a referenced clause, or normative content such as a requirement or recommendation applies, is in scope, is included or excluded, is subject to an applicability condition, or is excepted from an applicability rule. Do not infer clause-or-requirement applicability merely because a normative sentence contains technical conditions, prerequisites, guards, thresholds, or operating circumstances. A statement such as "when condition X holds, perform action Y" or "credit may be taken only if criterion Z is met" can constrain how a requirement is carried out without governing whether that requirement applies. Likewise, merely referring to another clause or requirement does not make that referenced content applicable; the statement must explicitly govern whether it applies.
